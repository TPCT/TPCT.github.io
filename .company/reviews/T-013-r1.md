## Blocking issues

1. **`src/styles/55-timeline.css` (≥1024 block, `.tl__item + .tl__item { margin-block-start: -40px; }`): at desktop, every visible year label collides with the previous card.**
   - In the alternating layout, item *i*'s year sits in the same column as item *i−1*'s card. The −40px interleave starts item *i* 40px above the previous card's bottom edge. The year starts 18px into the item, so its 38px line box overlaps the previous card's bottom border by 22px.
   - Measured at 1440 for all 9 visible years (2017, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026; same-year `vh` ones excluded): `prevCard.bottom − year.top = 22px` for each one.
   - Visually, the card's bottom border and rounded corner run through the digits ("2022", "2025", "2026"). This happens in dark and light, and with or without motion. See `.company/qa/shots/T-013-seq-2.png`, `T-013-seq-4.png`, `T-013-overlap-zoom-1440.png` and `T-013-full-1440-light-rm.png`.
   - This is a flaw in the ux-wow §6.5 interleave maths, not a transcription error, but it ships as broken visuals.
   - **Fix:** inside `@media (min-width: 1024px)` in `55-timeline.css`, change `.tl__item + .tl__item { margin-block-start: -40px; }` to `.tl__item + .tl__item { margin-block-start: -8px; }` and update its comment to `/* interleave; year (top 18px) must clear the previous card's bottom */`.
   - The year then starts ≥10px below the previous card. The node and connector (top 26/36px) stay aligned with the year. Change nothing else.
   - QA must re-check at 1024 and 1440 that no `.tl__year` (non-`vh`) intersects the previous `.tl__card` rect, and that no two cards overlap.
