### T-023
1. `src/scripts/80-bold.js:340` — Lenis anchor scroll applies the header offset twice. Lenis 1.3.27 `scrollTo(element)` already subtracts the root `scroll-padding-top` (92px here, from `10-base.css:19`) and the target's `scroll-margin-top` (see `lenis.min.js`: `m = rect.top + animatedScroll - scrollMarginTop - scrollPaddingTop`). Measured with real mouse clicks at 1440x900: `#timeline` top = 184px (baseline native scroll: 92px), `#about` 184px (baseline 92). Regression versus today's anchor landing.
   Fix: drop the `offset` option, i.e. `L.scrollTo(target,{duration:1.0,easing:t=>1-Math.pow(1-t,4)});` (keep `preventDefault`, `pushState`, no `stopPropagation`). `headerH()` stays in `TPCT_BOLD` (later modules use it). Add a comment: `// Lenis honours scroll-padding-top itself`. Verify in the browser check: after clicking each header nav link (`#about`, `#timeline`, `#skills`, `#services`) with `page.mouse.click` on the link's box, `target.getBoundingClientRect().top` is within 92 ± 4 px (same as native).
