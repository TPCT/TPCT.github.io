## New finding
### R2-B1 (blocker): mouse clicks on rail card links are swallowed
- **Cause:** `focusin` now always scrolls to the card's target position. A mouse press on a link focuses it first (mousedown → focusin), and the rail then jumps. The mouseup lands on a different element, so the `click` either goes to the wrong element or never reaches the link, and the link does not open.
- **Repro:** rail engaged at 1440×900, rail at 30% (or 60%). Press and release the mouse on "Project details" (JenDock) or "Read case study" (Ad-Break), when the card is not already at the left edge.
- **Event traces I recorded:**
  - At 30%: `mousedown: Project details @card-left 487` → `focusin` → `mouseup: Role Author… @225` → `click: Role Author…` (wrong element). `page.url()` stayed `/#work`; no navigation.
  - At 60%: the click target was `Case study / Analytics…` (another card).
  - Grid mode (reduced motion) as a control: mousedown, focusin, mouseup and click all land on the same link.
- **Impact:** for most cards, mouse and trackpad users can't open the project page from the rail.
- **Fix direction:** scroll only for keyboard focus, e.g. `if (!e.target.matches(':focus-visible')) return;` at the top of `focus`. Alternatively, set a flag on `pointerdown` inside the list and skip `focusin` while it is set. Keep the always-compute target `y` for keyboard focus, which is what fixed r1-B2.
- **Tests to add:** a browser or vm check that a pointer-initiated focus does not call `scrollTo`, and a re-run of the mouse click and Tab cases.

### Minor (no action required)
- **R2-m1:** with classic, non-overlay scrollbars (Chromium without `--hide-scrollbars`, 15px scrollbar), `100vw` makes the rail 8px wider than `clientWidth`, so `scrollWidth` is 1017 vs `clientWidth` 1009 at 1024. The page cannot be scrolled horizontally (`scrollX` stays 0, because body clips). The only effect is the rail edge sitting under the vertical scrollbar. Acceptable. An optional polish is to use the clientWidth instead of `100vw`.
